1Who we are

Thread & Dot (“the app”) is a puzzle game made by YR Tech, an independent developer based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India (“we”, “us”, “our”).

This policy explains, in simple words, what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2What is saved on your device

To remember your game, the app saves the following only on your phone:

  • Levels finished, stars, points, rank and challenge times
  • Daily-puzzle streak, lives and hints
  • The nickname and avatar you pick on the Profile screen
  • Settings such as sound, music, vibration, colour-blind mode and reminder time
We cannot see this data. It is not sent to us or to any server we run. The nickname never leaves your phone. We still suggest using a nickname, not a real full name.

Android backup

If Android Backup is turned on in your phone’s Google account settings, Android may include this game data in your device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is handled by Google under your Google account, not by us. You can turn it off in Android Settings → Google → Backup (the path can differ by device).

3What we do not collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store or sell:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or home addresses
  • Photos, contacts, files, camera or microphone data
  • Precise (GPS) location
  • Your Android Advertising ID – the app is set up so it does not use it
  • Payment details – the app has no in-app purchases

The only outside service in the app is Google’s advertising service, explained next.

4Advertising

Thread & Dot is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Ads appear:

  • as a full-screen ad after every 10 levels, when you tap “Next level”, and
  • as a short reward video, only if you choose to watch one (for example, to get a free hint).

How we keep ads safe for everyone

Because children may play, we have set the app so that for every user:

  • Ad requests are marked as child-directed (also called “Tag for Child-Directed Treatment”).
  • No personalised or interest-based ads are shown, and no ad profile is built from your use of the app.
  • Ads are limited to the “G” (General audiences) content rating.
  • The Android Advertising ID is not used.

5Children and parents

Thread & Dot is suitable for all ages, including children. We take extra care because of this:

  • We do not ask children (or anyone) for personal information.
  • There is no chat, no messaging, no friend list and no way to contact other players.
  • There are no in-app purchases.
  • Ads are non-personalised and family-safe for all users (see section 4).

We aim to follow Google Play’s Families policy, India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (which protects children under 18), and, for players in other countries, laws such as the US COPPA and the EU/UK GDPR.

A note for parents and guardians

If you think your child has given us personal information in any way (for example, by email), please contact us using the details in section 12. We will delete it promptly. You can also delete all game data on the device with Settings → Reset progress in the app, or by uninstalling it.

6Notifications and sharing

Daily reminder (optional)

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a notification on your phone. No information is sent anywhere. Turn it off any time in the app’s Settings or in Android’s notification settings.

Share button

The Share button makes a picture of your solved puzzle on your phone and opens Android’s share menu. Nothing is sent unless you pick an app (for example WhatsApp) and send it yourself. Parents may wish to supervise sharing by younger children.

8Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal data is processed, to correct or delete it, to withdraw consent, and to complain. In India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 gives you rights including access, correction, erasure, grievance redressal and nominating another person to act for you.

Because we do not keep your data on our servers, you can use most of these rights directly:

  • Delete game data: Settings → Reset progress, or uninstall the app.
  • Stop reminders: turn off the reminder in Settings.
  • Data processed by Google for ads: see the Google Privacy Policy and your Google account privacy settings.
  • Anything else: write to us (section 12). We reply within 30 days.

9Keeping and deleting data

Game data stays on your phone until you reset it or uninstall the app. We do not keep copies. If you email us, we keep the email only as long as needed to answer you, and then delete it. Google keeps advertising data according to its own retention rules.

10Security

Data is stored inside the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read. Communication with Google’s ad servers is encrypted (HTTPS). No method is 100% secure, but we keep the data the app handles to the minimum needed.

11Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information (for example, by adding a new feature), we will update this page and the “Last updated” date at the top. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app’s Play Store listing.
