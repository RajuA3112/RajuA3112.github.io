Quick answers

You might find your answer here before writing to us.

I ran out of lives. When do they come back?

One life comes back every 20 minutes, up to 5. Levels 1–5 never cost lives, so you can always keep practising there while you wait.

How do I get more hints?

You get up to 3 free hints, and one comes back every 30 minutes. When you are out, you can watch a short video to get one more. Hints from videos never expire.

Why didn’t my level count as solved?

Every pair of dots must be connected and every cell on the board must be filled. Dark tiles are walls and don’t need filling. The “Filled” counter above the board shows how many cells are still empty.

I got a new phone. Will my progress move with me?

Progress is saved on your phone, not on our servers. If Android Backup is on in your Google account, Android can restore it when you set up the new phone with the same account.

How do I delete my game data?

Open the app and go to Settings → Reset progress, or simply uninstall the app. We don’t keep a copy. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I play without internet?

Yes. All levels and the daily puzzle work offline. You only need internet to watch a video for a free hint.
